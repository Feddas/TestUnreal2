What is the Game's Title?
A: Liquid Wars AR

What Genre would you call it?
A: AugmentedReality RealTimeStrategy

Who do you want to play it?
A: 2 or more friends with their own smartphones capable of running ARKit or ARCore that can meet in person.

What's a one-sentence reason why someone should play it?
A: An AR sport to exercise both your brain and muscles, at the same time.

What is the emotion or experience you really want the player to have?
A: Competitive

What actions does the player do repeatedly?
A: Run around a room and point their phone camera.

What’s the player trying to achieve?
A: Filling the room with their teams color.

How does the player win or lose?
A: The player wins by coloring 80% of the room their color.

How does the game progress?
A: Each player starts with the same size cloud of particles surrounding them. Similar to how it is done in https://www.gnu.org/software/liquidwar6/ .

What actions or abilities does the player or their character have?
A:
Move around the room
Point their phone for vision.

What are all the controls?
A:
Physical meat space movement.
Physically moving a phone to use the screen as a window into an AR world.

What are the challenges the player or the character have to overcome?
A: Move to a position that allows their color cloud to win in combat against opponents color clouds.

How does the game measure the player's progress?
A: Percent size of color cloud size relative to other players

How do other objects or characters in the game react to the player or their character?
A: A players color cloud tries to follow that player. A cloud can’t go through walls. A cloud particle initiates combat whenever it contacts a particle of another color. When a particle is surrounded by another color, it dies faster.

Does the game have a story? What is it? Where does your level take place within that story?
A: No story. The closest to a story likely associated with this game would be player initiated tournament brackets.

How does the game tell the story to the player?
A: No story. For the brackets, friends shouting at each other in-person in the room.

What does a final version of the game 'look' like to you?
A: Game renders only the particle clouds and color bars in https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_War . The background is replaced with what the phones camera sees as pass-through.

What are the environments in the game like?
A: The color cloud is a mass of particles that share the same color. Bright when they’re at full health, dark when they’re about to die.

Does the game have UI? What information does it tell the player?
A: A bar along the side of the screen shows the percentage of each color cloud for each player playing the game.

Does the game have music? What kind and why?
A: The winning player’s phone plays something akin to “We Are the Champions” by Queen. So that the other players know who to destroy.

Does the game have sound effects? What do they communicate to the player?
A: No, just the king player sound track.

Do your characters speak? How do they speak? Does the player or the character hear them speak?
A: Players speak any way they’d like in-person. Particles in the game do not speak and are inaudible.
